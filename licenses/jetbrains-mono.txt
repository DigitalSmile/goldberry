The SIL Open Font License 1.1 is a long text with specific reserved-font-name
provisions. It is not reproduced from memory here, because an inaccurate licence
text is worse than an absent one.

Vendor it verbatim from the upstream OFL.txt at the pinned revision. The
canonical text is also published at https://openfontlicense.org.

OFL obligations that affect Goldberry:
  - The font may be bundled and redistributed with software.
  - The font may NOT be sold on its own.
  - This licence and its copyright notice must accompany the font.
  - If a Reserved Font Name is declared upstream, a modified version must be
    renamed. Goldberry ships these fonts unmodified, so this does not currently
    apply -- but it would if the fonts were ever subset or re-hinted.
