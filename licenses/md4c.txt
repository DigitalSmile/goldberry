Bundled as:    compiled into libgoldberry (src/md4c.c and src/entity.c)

Two translation units rather than a library: the superbuild compiles md4c's
parser and its HTML5 named-entity table straight into the shim's target, and
neither exports a symbol of its own -- the five `goldberry_md_*` entries on
the export list are Goldberry's (ADR-0294). The compiled code is present in
every binary artifact regardless, which is the redistribution this notice is
about.
