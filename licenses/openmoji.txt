MODIFICATION DISCLOSURE, required by CC BY-SA 4.0 section 3(a)(1)(B):

  Goldberry ships a derivative of OpenMoji. The COLRv0 colour variant's CPAL
  palette has been re-themed toward the Nord palette (docs/ARCHITECTURE.md
  §6.2). The monochrome (Black) variant is shipped unmodified.

OBLIGATIONS this creates, which are stricter than every other licence here:

  1. ATTRIBUTION -- credit the OpenMoji project, link the licence, and state
     that changes were made. Satisfied by NOTICE and this file, and it must
     also appear in any About dialog Goldberry ships.
  2. SHARE-ALIKE -- the re-themed font is itself licensed CC BY-SA 4.0 and must
     be distributed under those terms. It cannot be relicensed Apache-2.0.
  3. NO ADDITIONAL RESTRICTIONS -- Goldberry's own terms must not restrict
     what CC BY-SA permits for the font.

Share-alike reaches the derivative FONT only. It does not reach Goldberry's
Java or native code: the font is an embedded asset, and CC BY-SA has no
linking or combination clause of the kind copyleft software licences use.
