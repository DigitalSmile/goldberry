--- No reference text ---

HarfBuzz does NOT use the standard MIT text. It uses its own permissive
licence, commonly called "Old MIT", and its COPYING file carries a long list
of distinct copyright holders (Behdad Esfahbod, Google, Red Hat, Mozilla,
Codethink, and others) accumulated over the project's history.

For that reason no reference text is reproduced here: paraphrasing it, or
substituting standard MIT, would misstate the licence. Copy COPYING verbatim
from the pinned revision.
