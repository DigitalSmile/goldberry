Bundled as:    statically linked into libgoldberry (the `webpdecoder` target)

The decoder half only. The superbuild switches off the encoder, the muxer, the
animation demuxer, the near-lossless path and all nine command-line tools, so
what is linked is `webpdecode`, `webpdspdecode` and `webputilsdecode` and
nothing else. Three symbols reach Java -- WebPGetInfo, WebPDecodeRGBA and
WebPFree -- bound directly with no C glue, which is the same arrangement every
other upstream here has (ADR-0329).
