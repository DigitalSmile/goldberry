Ported, not copied: EASU and RCAS from ffx-fsr/ffx_fsr1.h, and the three
approximations they use from ffx-fsr/ffx_a.h, rewritten in HLSL in 32-bit

ship in the jar. The arithmetic is AMD's, which is the redistribution this
