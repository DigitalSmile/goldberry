# Review — 2026-09-18

A whole-repository review of code, tests and documentation: every `src/main`
and `src/test` tree, the build, CI, `README.md`, `docs/`, `book/src` and the
ADR index. Nothing in the tree was changed by the review itself; this file is
the record, and each entry below is meant to become one fix, one test, or one
line of prose. The status column at the end says what has been done about each.

> **What was done about it is in
> [`review-2026-09-18-fixes.md`](review-2026-09-18-fixes.md)**, entry by entry,
> with the ADRs each decision produced — and with the dozen places this document
> turned out to be wrong, which are kept rather than quietly corrected here. The
> text below is left exactly as it was written, because a review that is edited
> to match what happened is no longer evidence of anything.

## 0. How it was done

Twelve scoped reads (core in three parts, widgets in two, html, natives and the
weaver, build and example, docs, a repo-wide test-quality sweep, a repo-wide
hygiene sweep), each verifying its own claims against the source, followed by a
second verification here of every item that made the list: reproduced with a
small program against `build/classes` where a claim was behavioural (marked
*repro*), or re-read at the cited lines where it was structural (*read*).
`./gradlew check` is green (all tasks cached from the same tree). PMD reports
nothing; SpotBugs, which does not gate (ADR in `goldberry.java-conventions.gradle`
§SpotBugs), was triaged by hand — see §9.

The full suite was not re-run from clean; the flakiness notes in §6 come from
reading the tests, and from `FrameBudgetTest`'s own history.

## 11. Second pass over the tests: what pins the permanent, the obvious, or the upstream

### 11.4 Judged and kept, against the brief

`TimeTicksTest.februaryIsNotThirtyDays`/`daylightSavingIsNotADay` assert that ticks step in calendar units (every tick the 1st at 00:00, every tick local midnight across a spring-forward), which millisecond arithmetic would break — not `java.time` facts. `MessageTest.everyKindHasItsOwnGlyph`'s distinctness sweep is a §1.2 policy over a growing enum (only its second assertion is a literal). `RecordWitherTest` catches positional-argument slips across 45 wither lists. Every `isAnimating` test beside a declaration is *required* by `arch/AnimationSweepTest` (`CalendarTest:410`, `SkeletonTest:100`, `TourTest.theArrivalAsksForFrames`, `CarouselTest.Arriving`). `FrameSummaryTest.describe` pins a string a workflow greps (ADR-0342). `SparklineTest.theMarkerIsRound` is not subsumed by its golden — a square-vs-disc marker is ~8 px of 13,600, under the 2 % cap. `LogsTest.fallsBackToNoOpLoggers` is the only guard that `:common` binds no logging provider.

### 11.5 The coverage finding that outweighs the deletions

`ChainingTest.everyWidgetIsAttributed`, `ImmutabilityTest.catalog()` and `WidgetParityTest.builtIns()` all enumerate `Primitives.builtInTypes() + Controls.controlTypes()` — **24 names of the 74 registered**. `stack`, `qr-code` and every widget under `form`, `panel`, `menu`, `nav`, `overlay` and `data` are outside the parity, immutability and chaining sweeps. `SemanticsSweepTest` and `AnimationSweepTest` read the source tree and are complete; the other three should walk `Widgets.inflater().registered()` or `SourceTree` the same way. `DensityTest.SIZED` likewise names four of the eight controls with a §3 height. This is one change and it is worth more than every deletion above.
